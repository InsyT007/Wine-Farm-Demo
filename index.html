The Estate

Distinctive agriculture, rooted in Cape terroir.

Cape Town Farm is an integrated agricultural estate where centuries-old farming heritage, modern soil telemetry, and compassionate stewardship converge in one living landscape.

Sheltered by granite mountain slopes and cooled by south-westerly Atlantic breezes, our microclimates allow vineyards, sunlit citrus orchards, multi-species pastures, and grain rotations to thrive in biological harmony.

450+ Hectares Encompassing vineyards, orchards, rotational pastures, and indigenous fynbos reserves.
100% Water Stewardship Automated soil moisture probes, drip micro-irrigation, and closed-loop dam networks.
People-Centric Enterprise Over 120 permanent agricultural artisans, technicians, and operational specialists.

Sustainability & ESG

Our covenant with the land: leave every hectare richer than we found it.

At Cape Town Farm, sustainability is not marketing; it is our operational foundation. We combine traditional ecological knowledge with real-time digital environmental monitoring to protect soil, water, and wildlife.

💧 Water Stewardship

450kW solar telemetry probes schedule drip irrigation at night, reducing water consumption by 38%.

☀️ Solar Microgrid

The entire winery, packhouse, and workshop operate on a 450kW grid-tied solar photovoltaic array.

🦉 Biological Pest Control

Installed raptor perches and bat roosts maintain natural biological control over vineyard and orchard pests.

♻️ Zero Organic Waste

All grape pomace, citrus prunings, and livestock manure are composted into organic soil amendment.
